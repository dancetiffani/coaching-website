I'm not here to condemn or shame you for the meat you grew up eating over the course of your life. Most of us had no idea that delicious, finger-licking-good dinner plate at the table, the one your mouth watered for at the very sight, could have been some of the same food that, over time, may aid in causing our bodies to show symptoms of retaliation on our health.

But guess what?! Neither did I!!!

My story

I started my journey in college, my junior year, in 2002. I began by completely eliminating red meat abruptly from my diet after hearing about Mad Cow disease on the news. The very next year, my mom unexpectedly died, and following in my sister's footsteps, I decided to take matters of my daily health into my own hands.

It wasn't easy. Options and alternatives weren't readily available over 20 years ago like they are today. But I was determined to change my diet... and I did!

Maybe you've been telling yourself:

  • Me VEGAN? I could NEVER give up meat
  • I wanna be vegan but my family said they are NOT giving up meat! No way. How can I have a vegan and meat eating household?
  • Can I still live a normal life and eat plant based and vegan?
  • I wanna eat healthy. Is vegan food gross?

So what does this mean? What now?

  • How do you know what to trust is a healthy choice vs. an unhealthy choice?
  • Can you ever seriously eat guilt-free, tasty meals again, as good as those your mother cooked since childhood?
  • Is there a way to attend gatherings and eat around your non-meat-eating friends and not feel "awkward"?

Relax... the answer is YES!

I help people discover their own path toward a plant-based lifestyle, at their own pace, which comfortably suits them. We will teach you long-lasting changes that will help improve your way of thinking, eating, and living, to get you closest to achieving optimal health without feeling as though you are "forever missing out" or "giving up your soul" to be healthy!

Our goal is to formally educate clients on the vegan and plant-based lifestyle in an exciting way, so that it is a doable and sustainable long-term part of your everyday life... for life.

Questions, answered

Do I have to be 100% vegan to work with you?

No. Coaching is open to people at every point in their journey: vegan-curious, mostly vegan, fully vegan, or anywhere in between.

What if I've tried veganism before and it didn't stick?

Starting again is completely normal. Most lasting changes take more than one attempt, and one imperfect stretch doesn't erase your journey.

What about restaurants, travel, and my family?

Real life is exactly what we work on. Your family doesn't have to become vegan for you to start, and most menus have more options than they first appear to.

Do you offer phone or text support?

Yes. One month of phone, email and text support is available as a $60 add-on to any package. It's for coaching check-ins, not emergencies. Hours and response times are shared when you enroll.
